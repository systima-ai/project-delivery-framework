# Relationship health card workflow

Produces `_pdf-output/engagements/{active}/05-governance/relationship-health-cards/<YYYY-MM-DD>.md`.

**Output format:** dual view — summary table at top + narrative per sub-dimension. Both views in the same file. Same anatomy as `pdf-sdlc-health` and `pdf-qa-health`; different subject (the client relationship, not the codebase).

**What it measures.** The health of the working relationship with the client and key stakeholders, as a leading indicator. A relationship usually deteriorates before delivery metrics do; this card surfaces the early signals while they are still cheap to address.

**Models used (all public).** The sub-dimensions adapt the trust-equation from *The Trusted Advisor* (Maister, Green and Galford, 2000): credibility, reliability and intimacy build trust, self-orientation erodes it. Provenance and the per-dimension rubric are in `references/relationship-evidence-guide.md`. No model here is vendor-specific.

**Cadence:** on-demand + default every 4 weeks (overridable per engagement). Run it sooner when a warning sign appears.

## Preconditions

- `CHARTER.md` at revision ≥ 1
- `STAKEHOLDERS.md` populated. A `STAKEHOLDER-MAP.md` (from `pdf-map-stakeholders`) is recommended, since the Manage-Closely stakeholders are the natural subjects of this card.

## Intent: create

1. **Choose the subject.** *"Is this card for the engagement relationship as a whole, or for one named stakeholder? If the map exists, I'll default to the Manage-Closely group."*
2. **Offer source-mode.** *"Anything I can read first — recent 1:1 notes, the last steering minutes, a CSAT/NPS result, a call debrief? Paths welcome. If not, I'll interview you. [paths / interview]"*
3. **Read paths if provided.** Surface evidence per sub-dimension.
4. **Walk the six sub-dimensions** per the evidence guide. For each:
   - Show what evidence exists (if any was read).
   - Ask the canonical question.
   - Probe for a specific behaviour, not a feeling ("they cancelled the last two 1:1s" beats "they seem disengaged").
   - Reach a RAG judgement with the user.
   - Capture: headline finding, the warning sign or strength, recommended action (concrete, owned, deadlined where possible).
5. **Synthesise overall RAG**: overall = worst of sub-dimensions, **unless** the user explicitly justifies a higher overall rating (one isolated RED with action in flight). Justification must be recorded.
6. **Compose:**

```markdown
---
artifact_type: relationship-health-card
engagement: <slug>
date: <YYYY-MM-DD>
subject: <"engagement" | stakeholder ID/name>
overall_rag: <R|A|G>

schedule:
  default_cadence_days: 28
  next_default: <YYYY-MM-DD>

---
```

7. **Cross-skill side effects.**
   - For each RED sub-dimension, offer `pdf-update-raid` (append risk) with the warning sign as the risk title.
   - If overall is RED, offer `pdf-write-escalation-memo` (raise it deliberately) or `pdf-write-stakeholder-update` (reset expectations), Helena's call.
   - A RED on **Client-Centred Orientation** or **Candour** is a partnership risk, not just a delivery one; recommend a `pdf-prep-1on1` with the stakeholder (Iris) to address it directly.
8. **Write the file.** Record next default check date.

## Intent: update

Cards are date-stamped and append-only as a series; an `update` is a new card for a new date with a "compared to last check" line pre-populated when a prior card for the same subject exists. The prior card stays unchanged. A worsening trend across two consecutive cards is itself a finding to call out.

## Intent: validate

- [ ] Frontmatter complete; subject set; overall_rag set; next_default present
- [ ] Summary table has all six sub-dimensions
- [ ] Every sub-dimension has a narrative section with evidence / warning-sign-or-strength / action
- [ ] If overall RAG differs from worst sub-dimension, a justification paragraph is present
- [ ] Findings cite behaviours, not feelings, in the evidence fields
- [ ] Any named action owner exists in `STAKEHOLDERS.md`
- [ ] Sources list is non-empty

## Intent: dump-merge

Accept dumped material (a tense email thread, steering minutes, a CSAT verbatim, a 1:1 note). Map signals to sub-dimensions. Surface dimensions where evidence is thin and elicit those by Q&A. Do not infer a RED from a single bad interaction; look for pattern.

## Anti-patterns to refuse

- **Feelings as evidence.** "They seem unhappy" is not evidence; "they raised the SoW twice this week and shortened the last two reviews" is.
- **Green overall on the strength of politeness.** A cordial relationship with cancelled 1:1s and no candour is not GREEN.
- **A single incident driving the card.** One difficult call is a data point, not a trend. State the pattern or mark the dimension AMBER pending more signal.
- **Overall RAG that hides a RED sub-dimension.** Justify explicitly or the workflow flags it.
